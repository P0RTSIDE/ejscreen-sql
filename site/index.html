EJScreen SQL

Independent SQL reconstruction of EPA EJScreen 2.3

Rebuilds demographic indexes, percentiles, and EJ indexes in SQL from the archived 2024 block group tables, then compares them with the published Harvard extracts. Not affiliated with EPA. Not an official EPA product.

Try a sample query

Client-side playground over a 250 block group sample from the Harvard archive of the 2024 tables. This is a slice of the reconstruction reference, not EPA EJScreen and not a full national API. There is no live database behind this page. Values are not live EPA distances. In Simple filters, "near" means a high proximity or percentile-style score in this sample, not a custom mile radius.

Pick everyday topics. Thresholds are measured inside this reconstruction sample only. RMP facility proximity, linguistic isolation, and age shares are not in this extract, so those topics are omitted here.
